# MrQR - QR Forms for Field Teams

MrQR helps construction, facilities maintenance, and cleaning teams connect
physical places and equipment to useful digital workflows. Create QR codes that
take people to the right forms and information, then manage submissions and
related records in one place.

Use MrQR to build forms for field teams, share them with a QR code, and organize
the information your team collects. Learn how MrQR works for
[construction teams](https://about.mrqr.me/mrqr-on-the-building-site),
[facilities maintenance](https://about.mrqr.me/facilities-maintenance), and
[cleaning contractors](https://about.mrqr.me/cleaning-contractors).

## About

MrQR is a QR code and form platform for teams working across sites, facilities,
and service locations. It brings QR-linked forms, submissions, and related
team records together to support practical field workflows.
